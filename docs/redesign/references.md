# Reference analysis

Five client references (screenshot of 2026-11-10). We synthesize a shared DNA from them; we don't copy any one of them.

**Shared DNA:** an illustrated landscape as the page "stage" (hero + footer), a muted atmospheric palette with one warm accent, calm centered compositions with lots of air, an elegant serif with a clean body face, and illustration that means something (a metaphor, a character living in the scene).

| # | Reference | What it contributes | What we deliberately don't take |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Quiet Place**: pixel pine forest + lake, sage/cream, coral CTA, rabbit in the scene | The calm, centered hero; *one* coral CTA against sage; 3 small feature blocks under the hero; a mascot who **lives in** the scene, not in a fixed screen corner (Empanadillo gets a spot on the shore). | Pixel-art scenery (it would clash with vector layers and blur on retina), monospace type, toy-like proportions. |
| 2 | **Fantasy Valley**: flat vector mountains, lavender/slate/navy | Layered depth and atmospheric haze ("distance" = the journey), pill-shaped controls, dusty lavender far layer. | Bold geometric uppercase headline (too loud for a school), the search bar as the hero action, fantasy creatures, cold navy dominance. |
| 3 | **Not Nahid**: cream paper, serif, bookshelf navigation | Warm paper background and elegant serif (continues today's identity); an object as a navigation metaphor; small floating label chips (we label scene landmarks: "Spāņu valoda", "Angļu valoda"). | The literal bookshelf (too "library", and it doesn't fit a travel concept); the near-empty page with almost no content. |
| 4 | **Heritage Architecture**: peach sky, teal-slate silhouettes, one orange, refined serif | The palette logic: a muted world with **one ember accent**; peach dawn gradient; refined serif title over the sky; small-caps nav labels; cinematic calm. | The dark, heavy overall tone and the fairy-tale castle (too dramatic, and not tied to Spain/England or Latvia). |
| 5 | **TrafficBot footer**: dark forest silhouette + lookout tower into footer | The footer as a "landing": pines rise from the page into a dark multi-column footer; a lookout tower (*skatu tornis* is a real Latvian landmark type). | Saturated green, dense legal row and payment logos, generic corporate column labels. |

## How it maps to Libertas
- The logo already draws a **mountain line and two birds**, so the scene is the logo "opened up". The birds return in the sky as guides.
- **Baltic start → foreign horizons:** pine shore and lake in front (home, Latvia), a Spanish sierra with a windmill and English chalk cliffs with a lighthouse on the horizon (the destinations).
- Tagline "Runā, saproti, ceļo, audz!" = shore → path → horizon → young pines.
